## Visual identity
* **Colour:** signature turquoise / aqua (logo circle ≈ #45DDE0, labels ≈ #22BDE8) with deep ink text.
* **Logo:** serif wordmark "ROSA ALBA" under a round ornamental rosette monogram (RA). → used for the store palette (`brand-*`, `ink`, cream, gold accent).
* **Packaging:** aqua label on clear/frosted glass; dropper bottle with silver collar (serum), frosted jar with brushed-silver lid (night cream), white tube with ornamental band (mask), clear tube (gel), pump spray bottle (dry oil).
* **Photography:** clean lifestyle flat-lays — towels, pool/water splash, almonds, prickly pear, green leaves, a daisy; hands holding the tube on a mint background. Locations tagged Marrakech / Maroc.

## Gaps & opportunities (feeds Phase 2)
1. **Ordering friction:** WhatsApp only, no prices, no product page → a COD store with a 30-second form removes most of it.
2. **No social proof visible** (no reviews/UGC/before-after in the screenshots) → collect real reviews fast (WhatsApp screenshots, unboxing videos).
3. **Strong natural-Moroccan-oils story** (argan + figue de Barbarie) that is under-used: ingredient education and "routine" bundles (serum + night cream + mask).
4. **Bilingual market:** keep Arabic/Darija for reach, French for premium positioning.
5. **Compliance:** several captions make treatment claims (anti-wrinkle, removes pigmentation). Meta/TikTok may reject such ads → phrase as "aide à atténuer l'aspect…", avoid before/after of skin conditions.
